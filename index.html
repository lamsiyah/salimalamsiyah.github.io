I am a researcher at the University of Luxembourg, working on natural language processing and machine learning. My research focuses on explainable and responsible NLP, explainable AI (XAI) for machine translation, AI-generated text detection, and generative AI for education.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Ibn Tofail University in 2021, co-supervised with Aix-Marseille University. My doctoral research addressed deep learning methods for unsupervised multi-document summarization.

Alongside my research, I teach Natural Language Processing and supervise Bachelor’s and Master’s research projects. I am an Associate Editor of Natural Language Processing (Cambridge University Press) and a member of the advisory board of John Benjamins’ Natural Language Processing book series.

Research interests

  • Explainable and responsible NLP: model interpretability, attribution methods, explainable AI (XAI) for machine translation, faithfulness evaluation, and accountability.
  • Generative AI for education: educational question generation, adaptive tutoring, AI-generated text detection, and student-centred learning.
  • Text summarization and retrieval: multi-document and long-document summarization, retrieval-augmented generation, and historical document analysis.
  • Low-resource language technologies: language understanding and resources for Arabic, Moroccan Darija, and Luxembourgish.

Research resources: SPARK · FairDetect · Faithfulness evaluation survey · NotAI.AI
