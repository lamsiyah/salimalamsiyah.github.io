A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Language, learning,
and the people
behind the models.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Luxembourg, where I combine research in natural language processing with teaching and student supervision.

My work spans explainable NLP, AI-generated text detection, text summarization, retrieval-augmented generation, and generative AI for education. I am particularly interested in the responsible development of language technologies and their use across languages and learning contexts.

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Ibn Tofail University, co-supervised with Aix-Marseille University. My earlier experience as a mathematics teacher continues to shape the way I teach, mentor, and approach educational AI.
